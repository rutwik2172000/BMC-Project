Cultural Norms at BMC

  1. Customer Congeniality - We are enthused by Customers. We keep away from gup-shup space, still we are nearby.
  2. Cooking the right way (pre/post - Safe & Hygiene) - We are obsessed by safety, security, and hygiene.
  3. Constancy Fulfillment of Brand - We consistently deliver to build brand loyalty. We are committed to deliver high-quality Ambience at a reasonable Value.
  4. Cuisine as per Customers' preferences - We urge to observe preferences to help in preparation of a desirable Menu of chai and quick food. We consider feedback as a Gift.
  5. Can Do - We can do, we do right every time. No compromise.
  6. Conceptualisation and Composition (development) - We promise to evolve and develop and outshine the Menu plus Service. Keep raising the benchmarks.
  7. Cognizance of hunger - We are conscious of wastage and advocate the same.
  8. Cache Inventory for best taste & shelf life - We keep our food and guests fresh.
  9. Cheer up differences - We encourage voice difference of opinions, thoughts, ways of working which is beginning of Consensus building.
  10. Control & Correct (deviations) - We have zero tolerance to deviations from laid down SOPs and Recipes. Be consistent in following the tested and proven norms.
  11. Consider yourself the Owner - We empower you to think, take decisions, compliment each other, solve problems, learn from mistakes and be accountable as you are the Owner.
  12. Challenges are to overcome - Nothing is easy, nothing is impossible. Believe in the power of persistence to push forward from 99°C to 100°C.
  13. Champion equality - Rise above gender, creed, or hierarchy levels. “Concept to Action” makes you Superior.
  14. Code of Conduct is sacrosanct - Being high on integrity, honesty, mutual respect, fair to oneself, others and organisation makes oneself rise high. Shortcuts are stumble prone.
